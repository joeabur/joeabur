About

Security operations, connected to engineering

I’m a cybersecurity analyst with hands-on SOC experience across threat detection, alert triage, incident response, and vulnerability management in cloud and hybrid environments.

My day-to-day work covers SIEM detection engineering, endpoint and edge monitoring with CrowdStrike Falcon and Cloudflare, and log correlation across AWS CloudTrail, CloudWatch, Kubernetes, and container workloads. I take incidents from initial triage through to post-incident review, then feed what I learn back into detection tuning and reporting.

I care about the space between security operations and engineering: defining logging standards, supporting security checks in CI/CD, tracking remediation, and working with engineering and infrastructure teams so that findings turn into real improvements.

I have working knowledge of ISO 27001, NIST CSF, PCI DSS, and SOC 2.
