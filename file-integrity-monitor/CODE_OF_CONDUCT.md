# Code of conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Our pledge.** We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

**Expected behavior.** Be respectful and constructive; give and accept review feedback
graciously; focus on what is best for users and the project; assume good faith.

**Unacceptable behavior.** Harassment, insults, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others'
private information, and any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
professional setting.

**Security research.** Good-faith, coordinated vulnerability reports (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) are welcome. Attacking systems you do not own is not.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code and
may ban contributors for repeated or severe violations. Report concerns to
markjoejay1960@gmail.com; reports are handled confidentially.
