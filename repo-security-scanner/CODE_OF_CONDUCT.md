# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(licensed CC BY 4.0). The commitments below are a short statement of it.

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, assume good faith and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Accept feedback gracefully; credit others' work.
- Keep security discussions responsible: report vulnerabilities privately (see `SECURITY.md`) and never
  post real credentials, personal data or exploit material targeting systems you do not own.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal or political attacks, doxxing,
sustained disruption of discussions, sexualised language or imagery, and any conduct that would
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban
participants for behaviour they judge inappropriate. Report concerns privately to the maintainers via
the contact on the repository profile; reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who are
involved in an incident recuse themselves from deciding it.

This policy applies in all project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
