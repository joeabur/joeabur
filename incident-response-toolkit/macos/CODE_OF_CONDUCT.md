# Code of Conduct

We follow the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussion focused on defensive, authorised security work. Requests for help attacking systems you do not
  own or are not authorised to test will be closed.
- Do not post real victim data, credentials, or production evidence in issues or pull requests.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer (see `SECURITY.md` for contact guidance). Maintainers may remove
content or contributors that violate these expectations.
